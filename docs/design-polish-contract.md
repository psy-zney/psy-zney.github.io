# Spatial portfolio design contract

Current-build preservation requirements and regression commands are recorded
in [feature-preservation.vi.md](feature-preservation.vi.md). Keep the implemented
continuous Web experiences → Apps & tools chapters, product selectors, optional live preview, wave loader and
black-hole workspace entry while refining this design. Earlier minimal-control
proposals below must not be used to remove these current features implicitly.

The viewer scrolls through a small universe, discovers one short piece of information per camera angle, turns a ring of real product previews and reaches a doorway into the existing workspace. Minimal visible controls and concise captions are the user's latest direction; it supersedes the earlier projection-wall layout.

References: the supplied screenshots and Studio Mirage's inspected projection scene inform the scale of the media and perspective neighbors. Aengel's immersive scene returns an error in this environment, so its motion is not claimed as verified. Do not copy reference assets.

Keep one signature, one quiet scroll hint and a mute icon. Remove the Screening room/Explore pills, map/menu controls, station and page counters, dense about/skills panels, torn caption borders, previous/next buttons and numbered gallery selectors. Contact contains only email, CV and the doorway action.

The camera follows a real 180-degree semicircle around each model at a constant radius. The two Porrima reading stops occupy the beginning and end of that semicircle. Text changes between upper-left, lower-right, upper-right and lower-left compositions. Reverse scrolling retraces the same camera pose and sentence. After the final Porrima sentence, scrolling opens the product ring automatically; scrolling past its final product continues to Skills, and reverse scrolling returns through the ring. Contact retains a front-facing view to keep the black-hole silhouette circular. Door entry keeps the existing workspace transition.

Product screens share one cylindrical arrangement. Vertical wheel, horizontal trackpad and vertical/horizontal touch drags continuously change its angle. Preserve drag velocity without mandatory snapping, suppress trailing drag clicks and let the nearest screen supply its name and direct visit/download action. A deliberate new scroll at either end continues the journey after settling; arrow keys can leave at the ends too. Home/End select the first/last product. Public hashes remain shareable and opening/closing a case study restores the product and focus.

The latest refinement enlarges the central preview and removes the gallery's X exit control. The primary way forward/back is scrolling through the flow. Keep full 16:9 previews and leave enough room for captions, selectors and actions in portrait and landscape.

Retain all nine supplied product addresses and the ten-case-study archive. Authentic bundled previews keep their full 16:9 composition. The user removed the central icon/title/placeholder card from Manager; its accessible missing-preview status, exterior caption and visit link remain. Product downloads open the supplied download landing pages. Loading, failed images and invalid product routes have readable states and recovery; no fabricated preview or automatic installer download.

Movement audio uses a quiet filtered air layer and soft tonal layer. Their envelopes follow camera/ring movement and doorway entry. Wait for a user gesture, support mute, silence hidden/paused/reduced-motion views, and clean up audio nodes/listeners on unmount. No standalone fracture or impact track.

Measure dimensions on resize, animate screens only while moving and render the gallery's 3D background on demand. Keep portrait and landscape compositions, 44px controls, keyboard access, real route/focus behavior and immediate flat-plane reduced-motion navigation. Verify 1440×900, 390×844 and 844×390 in the browser, then run portfolio, experience and build checks. Record simulated input and physical-device/audio limitations honestly in the evidence report.
